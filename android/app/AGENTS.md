Read [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) before changing how the wallet shows, stores or moves the
recovery phrase, ecash notes or the seed's origin, or before adding a screen that does. It lists the
rules (secure screens, nothing secret in saved state or logs, the clipboard, money moving once) and
where each is enforced. [`DECISION.md`](DECISION.md) records why the app is built the way it is.
